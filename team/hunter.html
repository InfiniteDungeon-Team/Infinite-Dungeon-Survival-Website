About Me

I’ve built my career across two parallel tracks—finance/accounting and technology. Today I work as a Senior Accountant at Central Connecticut Coast YMCA, where I combine analytical thinking with my passion for building interactive systems.

My long-term goal is to deepen my skills as a software engineer and game designer. Through this program I’ve gained experience with web development, system design, unit testing, and full-stack project work, while also refining my ability to scope features, debug complex issues, and collaborate across disciplines.

Role on Infinite Dungeon Survival

On Infinite Dungeon Survival, I worked as our primary gameplay programmer and systems designer. My contributions included:

  • Building the core twin-stick movement and shooting controls.
  • Designing and tuning enemy waves and spawn logic.
  • Creating the arena layout and encounter pacing.
  • Collaborating on balance changes and difficulty scaling across waves.
  • Supporting UI and feedback tuning based on playtesting with Elliotte and Chancler.
