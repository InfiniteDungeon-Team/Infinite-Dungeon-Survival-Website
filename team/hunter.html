About Me

I’ve built my career across two parallel tracks — finance/accounting and technology. Today I work as a Senior Accountant at Central Connecticut Coast YMCA, where I combine analytical thinking with my passion for building technology-driven solutions.

My interest in software engineering led me to pursue a Master of Science in Software Engineering. Through the program, I have gained experience in web development, system design, task automation, and full-stack project work that directly connects to business operations and workflow improvement.

Before transitioning into accounting, I spent several years as a game developer and co-owner of Telefrag Entertainment, LLC— where I collaborated on gameplay design, level layouts, pixel art, and mechanic implementation. This background continues to drive my passion for game design and interactive systems.
