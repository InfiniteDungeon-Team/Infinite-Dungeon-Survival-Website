Hunter Blake

I balance two tracks — finance/accounting and technology. On Infinite Dungeon Survival, I focus on enemy design, gameplay feel, and level pacing, bringing a structured, systems-thinking mindset to our game.

About Me

I currently work as a Senior Accountant at Central Connecticut Coast YMCA, where I handle general ledger management, financial reporting, and support for large-scale accounting operations. My interest in software engineering led me to pursue a Master of Science in Software Engineering, where I've gained experience in web development, system design, task automation, and full-stack project work.

Before that, I spent several years as a game developer and co-owner of Telefrag Entertainment, LLC, collaborating on level layouts, pixel art, combat mechanics, and overall game design. That background feeds directly into my work on Infinite Dungeon Survival.
