Mechanics & Level Design

Core Loop

  • Survive wave → earn credits → vendor phase → upgrade → next wave.
  • Twin-stick controls with responsive dodge & hit feedback.
  • Scaling enemy spawns and hazards increase difficulty.

Upgrades

  • Damage, Area-of-Effect, Health, and Utility (e.g., movement speed).

Level

Single sealed arena with four spawn gates and rotating hazards (lasers). Score = waves cleared + time survived.
