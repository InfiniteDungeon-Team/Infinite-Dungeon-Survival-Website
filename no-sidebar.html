Project Overview

Abstract

Infinite Dungeon Survival is a top-down twin-stick roguelike set in a dystopian arena called The Trials. Players survive timed waves, then enter a vendor phase to purchase damage, AoE, and health upgrades with credits earned from kills.

Design Goals

Deliver a responsive combat loop with readable feedback, scalable difficulty, and meaningful run-to-run decisions via an upgrade economy.

Level & Hazards

The game takes place in a single sealed arena with four entry points. Laser traps and crowd pressure force spatial awareness and movement planning.
