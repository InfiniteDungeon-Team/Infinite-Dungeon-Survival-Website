Highlights

Twin-Stick Combat

Controller & KBM friendly with responsive dodge and clear hit feedback.

Wave Scaling

Difficulty ramps each round; survive until the vendor phase to upgrade.

Arena Hazards

Laser traps constrain space, rewarding smart positioning and crowd control.

Core Features

Credits & Vendor

Earn credits from kills; buy damage, Area-of-Effect, health, or utility upgrades between waves.
