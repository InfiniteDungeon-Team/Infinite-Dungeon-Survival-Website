The Introduction

A fast, replayable survival arena built in Unity.

Survive escalating waves, earn credits, and upgrade between rounds.

Waves scale in difficulty, a vendor appears after each wave with damage/AoE/health upgrades, and hazards like laser traps keep movement tactical.

  • Read the Project
Highlights

Core Features

Designed for responsive controls, readable feedback, and a tight upgrade loop.

Twin-Stick Combat

Controller/KBM friendly; fast movement and shooting with clear hit feedback.

Wave Scaling

Difficulty ramps each round; survive until the timer to reach the vendor phase.

Credits & Vendor

Earn credits from kills; buy damage, AoE, or health upgrades between waves.

Arena Hazards

Laser traps constrain space, rewarding smart positioning and crowd control.
